WHAT'S THIS ABOUT

ONE PLATFORM. MANY CREATORS.
ENDLESS CONNECTIONS.

CONFLUENCE is more than just an event. It's a space created to bring Kerala's growing creator community and leading brands together. From content creators and influencers to photographers, videographers and creative minds, this is where people come together to share ideas, discover opportunities and build meaningful connections.

Behind every piece of content is a creative mind with a story to tell. CONFLUENCE brings these voices together and gives them a space to connect, grow and collaborate.

CONFLUENCE brings creators, brands and marketing professionals under one roof for a day of networking, conversations and collaboration.Expect creator meetups, brand connect sessions, expert discussions, interactive experiences and opportunities to discover new possibilities.

Whether you're creating content, building a brand or shaping the digital space, there's a connection waiting to happen here. CONFLUENCE — Connecting Creators, Brands & Communities..
